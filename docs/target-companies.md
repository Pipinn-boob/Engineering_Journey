## Entry routes (student-specific)

- **Industrial attachment** — mandatory for most Telecommunications degrees
  in Kenya. Start asking early (HR emails plus department notices), and
  bring this repository as evidence.
- **Internships and graduate trainee programs** — operators run annual
  intakes; deadlines matter.
- **Competitions** — Huawei ICT Competition (via the university) and
  hackathons, including Africa's Talking developer events.
- **Digital-skills programs** — Ajira Digital, the government program for
  young Kenyans.
- **Remote platforms** — Andela and similar talent networks once the
  portfolio has a few finished projects.
